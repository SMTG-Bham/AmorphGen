# Code of conduct

AmorphGen follows the Contributor Covenant, version 3.0:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/>.
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project, whether they use it,
contribute to it or maintain it.

In short: be welcoming and patient, criticise code and results rather than the
people behind them, and do not harass, insult or demean anyone. Many people who
open an issue here are new to molecular dynamics or to open-source software;
answer them as you would a colleague.

## Where it applies

- the issues, pull requests and code review of this repository;
- email and other messages about the project;
- anywhere you represent AmorphGen, such as a talk or tutorial given on its
  behalf.

## Reporting a problem

Email the maintainer, Chaiyawat Kaewmeechai, at c.kaewmeechai@bham.ac.uk.

The maintainers are the community moderators the Covenant refers to. They handle
reports as its "Reporting an Issue" and "Addressing and Repairing Harm" sections
describe, with responses that range from a private warning to a permanent ban
from the project's spaces.
